A photo gallery sits under the story text. On a phone, readers swipe sideways. On a computer, they can scroll, or press Tab to reach the gallery and use the arrow keys.

Every picture needs a one-sentence description in `alt`. It is read aloud to readers who cannot see the picture, and shown if the picture fails to load. Captions are optional.

Set `image` as well, so the story has a cover picture on the home page and in the Photos section.
